1. Privacy Policy (Политика конфиденциальности)

Welcome to ProDev ScanDoc ("Application", "we", "our", or "us"), provided by ProDevUz. We are committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring transparency about how your data is handled. This Privacy Policy explains how information is collected, used, and safeguarded when you use our mobile application (Package: uz.prodev.scandoc).

Key Summary: Your documents and scans belong entirely to you. We do not sell your personal data. Data is processed solely to provide document scanning, OCR text extraction, PDF generation, and subscription licensing.

1.1 Information We Collect

  • Account & Profile Information: When you sign in (e.g., via Google Sign-In), we receive basic profile details such as your email address, display name, and unique user identifier. This information is used strictly to identify your account and manage your scan quota and subscriptions.
  • Camera & Storage Data: To capture document pages, the app requests access to your device's camera. To save, export, and manage your scanned PDF files, the app requests access to device storage. Scans and images are processed on your device.
  • In-App Purchases & Subscription Data: When you purchase the Pro subscription, transaction data (such as Google Play purchase tokens and order IDs) is securely sent to our backend to verify and activate your Pro license. Payment details (e.g., credit card numbers) are processed exclusively by Google Play and are never accessible to us.
  • Crash & Performance Data: We may collect anonymous crash logs and diagnostics to ensure stability and improve application performance.

1.2 How We Use Your Information

  • To provide core features: multi-page scanning, image enhancement, OCR text recognition, and PDF generation.
  • To authenticate your user identity and enforce plan quotas (e.g. Free monthly scans vs. unlimited Pro plan).
  • To verify and maintain your Google Play active subscriptions.
  • To provide customer support and technical troubleshooting.

1.3 Data Storage and Security

All data transmitted between the Application and our servers is encrypted in transit using industry-standard Transport Layer Security (HTTPS/TLS). We implement strict security measures to protect against unauthorized access, alteration, or disclosure of user data.

1.4 Third-Party Services

  • Google Play Services / Google Sign-In: For secure user authentication.
  • Google Play In-App Billing: For secure subscription and payment processing.
  • Google ML Kit: For on-device Optical Character Recognition (OCR) and text recognition.
